# Section X Scoreboard Connected Sports Roadmap

## North Star
The first place fans check for everything Section X, and the permanent digital archive they return to years later.

## Phase A - Game Center foundation
- Permanent game pages (already exist)
- Connected photo submission
- Period scoring capability
- Sport-aware team/player stat capability
- Data provenance and verification

## Phase B - Game Center admin editor
- Score by period
- Sport-specific stat inputs
- Player box score from active roster
- Verification/source controls
- Featured game photo

## Phase C - Connected galleries
- Game galleries
- Team galleries
- School galleries
- Sport galleries
- Athlete tagging and athlete galleries
- Photographer/contributor credit pages

## Phase D - Team customization
- Team hero/team photo
- Overview, schedule, roster, stats, photos, history tabs
- Featured story/athlete/photo
- Program description and historical notes

## Phase E - Athlete careers
- Season-by-season teams
- Game and season stats
- Awards and Athlete of the Week
- Tagged photos
- Career totals and milestones

## Phase F - Leaders and records
- Section X leaders by sport/season
- School records
- Section X records
- Single-game, season, and career records
- Source citations/provenance for historical claims

## Phase G - Tonight in Section X
- Daily live hub
- Upcoming, live, final
- Game Center deep links
- Recent photos/recaps
- Standings movement and top performances

## Non-negotiables
- Sparse data must still look polished.
- Never require every coach to participate for the site to be useful.
- One submission should feed every relevant page.
- Preserve source and verification information.
- Existing schedules, standings, imports, team pages, and school hubs remain intact.
